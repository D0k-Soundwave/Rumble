## Section 1: Sound Preference Assessment (SP-HHQ Framework)
*7 core factors that predict user satisfaction — rate on 1–5 scale*

### F1: Annoyance/Distraction by Background Noise
- **Q1.1:** How bothered are you by background noise or distracting sounds?
  - Scale: 1 (Doesn't bother me) to 5 (Extremely bothered)
- **Q1.2 (Follow-up):** In what environments do you notice this most?
  - Options: restaurants, traffic, offices, crowds, [other]

### F2: Importance of Sound Quality
- **Q2.1:** How important is sound quality and audio fidelity to you?
  - Scale: 1 (Not important) to 5 (Extremely important)
- **Q2.2 (Follow-up):** What aspects matter most to you?
  - Options: clarity, detail, warmth, punch, smoothness, [other]

### F3: Noise Sensitivity (General)
- **Q3.1:** Do you have general sensitivity to loud or unpleasant sounds?
  - Scale: 1 (Very resilient) to 5 (Very sensitive)
- **Q3.2 (Follow-up):** Are there specific frequencies or sounds that annoy you?
  - Options: sibilance, high-pitched, boomy bass, [other]

### F4: Avoidance of Unpredictable Sounds
- **Q4.1:** Do sudden or unexpected sounds disturb you?
  - Scale: 1 (No, fine) to 5 (Yes, easily startled)
- **Q4.2 (Follow-up):** Would you prefer music with dynamic variation or more consistent volume?
  - Options: Dynamic variation, Consistent volume

### F5: Openness to Loud/New Sounds
- **Q5.1:** Are you open to exploring new music genres and bold, loud experimental sounds?
  - Scale: 1 (No, prefer familiar) to 5 (Yes, love exploring)
- **Q5.2 (Follow-up):** What's the loudest you typically enjoy listening?
  - Open-ended text response

### F6: Preference for Warm vs. Bright Tonal Balance
- **Q6.1:** Do you prefer warm, dark, full-bodied sound or bright, crisp, detailed sound?
  - Scale: 1 (Warm/dark) to 5 (Bright/crisp)
- **Q6.2 (Follow-up):** Describe your ideal tonal balance.
  - Open-ended text response (e.g., "Warm with presence peak in vocals")

### F7: Attention to Environmental Sound Details
- **Q7.1:** Do you focus on fine musical details and nuances, or prefer the overall impression?
  - Scale: 1 (Overall impression) to 5 (Fine details)
- **Q7.2 (Follow-up):** Do you listen critically or passively?
  - Options: Critically, Passively

- **Q8:** Do you have any hearing loss or auditory conditions?

## Section 4: Listening Style Profile
*Identify primary and secondary listening style*

**Q20: Which listening style best describes you?**
*(Can select primary + secondary)*

- **Appreciative Listener**
  - Description: "I listen for enjoyment and relaxation. I don't worry about technical details; I just want to feel good."
  - Prefers: Warm, inviting sound; emotional content; smooth playback

- **Empathic Listener**
  - Description: "I listen to connect emotionally with the artist/speaker. I want to feel what they're expressing."
  - Prefers: Balanced, natural sound that preserves emotion; clarity in vocals

- **Comprehensive Listener**
  - Description: "I listen to understand the structure and composition. I like to 'organize' what I hear."
  - Prefers: Well-separated instruments, clear layering, organized soundstage

- **Discerning Listener**
  - Description: "I listen to extract complete information and understand nuance. I want all the details."
  - Prefers: Transparent, accurate reproduction; detailed, natural sound

- **Evaluative Listener**
  - Description: "I listen critically to judge quality or make decisions. I'm analyzing what I hear."
  - Prefers: Accurate, reference-grade sound; ability to hear flaws/strengths

## Section 6: Correction Philosophy
*How aggressively should correction be applied?*

- **Q32:** How aggressively should correction be applied?
  - Options:
    - Conservative: Small boosts/cuts (±3 dB max), only correct major deviations
    - Moderate: ±5 dB range, balance playback quality with correction
    - Aggressive: ±8 dB or more, pursue specific tonal goals

- **Q33:** Should the profile compensate for headphone/speaker coloration?
  - Options:
    - Yes, aim for linear response
    - Partially, retain some of the equipment's character
    - No, enhance the equipment's inherent sound signature

- **Q34:** Is there a target reference curve you have in mind?
  - Options:
    - Harman Target (headphone, designed for preferred sound)
    - Crinacle Neutral (community consensus headphone target)
    - Diffuse-Field (studio standard for speakers)
    - Custom preference curve (describe in detail)
    - No preference, trust the AI to decide

## Administration Tips

1. **All sound preference questions are required** (F1–F7, Q1–Q14)
2. **Hearing & Medical section is critical** (Q8–Q13) — informs all EQ decisions
3. **Audio Habits provides context** (Q14–Q19) — baseline understanding
4. **Listening Style is classification** (Q20) — predicts EQ preferences
5. **Use Case section is required** (Q21–Q34) — determines profile direction
6. **Reference & Measurement optional but valuable** (Q35–Q37) — improves accuracy
7. **Listening Level is important** (Q38–Q40) — enables loudness compensation

## Notes for AI Implementation

- **Skip conditionality:** Only ask use-case questions relevant to the user's primary listening (based on Q18 activity breakdown)
- **Default to moderate correction** (Q32) if user is uncertain
- **Use SP-HHQ scoring** to weight profile decisions — higher sensitivity scores suggest warmer EQ
- **Combine listening style + use case** to determine final curve (e.g., Appreciative + Gaming = warm with gaming clarity focus)
- **Document all responses** — trace every profile decision back to questionnaire answers for validation
